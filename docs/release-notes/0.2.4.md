Passenger 0.2.4 is a hotfix to 0.2.3 with two fixes to what you see: a template's options in New Solution, New Project and New Item, and the text of the XAML preview. The rest is 0.2.3's: Add › New Item from any `dotnet new` item template, every option a template takes, Docker and Aspire set-up for a new service, and some 380 fixes, all in [0.2.3's release notes](https://github.com/Zaneris/passenger/releases/tag/v0.2.3). It is still free and open source under MIT.

## Fixed

- **Every option of a template has its name** in New Solution, New Project and New Item: an option the template gives no name of its own (a Web App's authentication and its Azure AD and B2C settings) showed an empty label beside its value, and its checkbox said nothing. The options a template picks for an IDE stay in view, and the rest of what `dotnet new` takes wait under More options, each named by its switch (`--auth`) with what it does beneath, the fold opening by itself where one of them is changed.
- **The XAML preview's text is as sharp as the app draws it** when the window previewed is larger than the pane: the previewer draws it smaller itself and the pane shows it pixel for pixel, where the full-size picture was shrunk, smearing its text (at 100% on Windows, ClearType's colour fringes into blotches).
